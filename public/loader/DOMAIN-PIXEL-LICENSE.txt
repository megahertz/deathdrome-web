Domain Pixel

The hand-authored bitmap glyph design in tools/build_installer_font.py and the
resulting domain-pixel.ttf are original project artwork, dedicated to the public
domain under CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

This font was drawn independently. It is not extracted from DeathDrome or
converted from the game's ZFO fonts. No original game resources are required
to display the installer. FontTools is used solely to generate the TTF file.
